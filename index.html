02 How I work
Adoption is the real product.

Building CAPS taught me the hardest part of an internal tool isn’t shipping it — it’s getting people to stop using their spreadsheets. Value proposition and adoption deserve the same rigor as features.

SQL before spec.

On the duplicate-payment API, the root cause wasn’t the matching algorithm — it was upstream payee-master data. I dig into the data before writing the spec, which is why fixes hold beyond launch.

I build, not just specify.

I ship AI-assisted products end-to-end with Claude Code, Figma, and GitHub — making the architecture and safety calls myself. Rapid prototyping with AI is how I work daily, not a bolt-on.

AI where it earns its keep.

At SmoothPaycheck I deliberately kept AI as a content-production tool, not a product feature, because the value was computational. Knowing where AI adds value versus noise is the call I make most.
